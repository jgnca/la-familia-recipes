Every recipe added to La Familia Recipe Book and every correction made to one, newest first.

## November 9, 2026

- **Book · Recipe book:** Added this change log, plus a History section on each recipe.
- **Changed · Roasted Delicata Squash Rings with Miso Butter:** The roast and flip steps now say when the pan goes in and comes out of the oven.
  - *Why:* Small gaps in the oven instructions.
- **Changed · Maple-Miso Glazed Whole Roasted Salmon:** The oven now starts heating with 15 minutes of marinating left (before, it started after). The glaze step says to take the pan out and return it.
  - *Why:* The salmon would otherwise wait 15 minutes for the oven.
- **Changed · Wild Rice Stuffing with Mushrooms, Cranberries and Pecans:** The oven preheat moved from the rice step to the mushroom step. The bake step now says to put the pot or dish in the oven uncovered.
  - *Why:* The oven was heating about 45 minutes before it was needed, and the bake step didn't say what goes in.
- **Changed · Ginger Miso Brothy Soup with Crispy Sesame Tofu:** Heating the oven is now its own step, and the tofu is seasoned while it heats. The toss step says to take the pan out and return it.
  - *Why:* The tofu went into the oven before it had time to heat.
- **Changed · Rosemary Sourdough Focaccia:** The oven preheat to 450°F moved to the final rise, about 30 minutes before it ends. Before, it came right before baking.
  - *Why:* A 450°F oven needs 20–30 minutes to heat, so the bread would have gone into a cold oven.
- **Changed · Lentil & Wild Rice Stuffed Artichoke Bottoms:** Added an oven preheat to 400°F at step 2 (it was missing). The broil step now says to switch the oven to broil and use the top third of the oven.
  - *Why:* The oven was never preheated, and the broil step was vague.
- **Changed · Crispy Smashed Potatoes with Garlic and Rosemary:** Heating the oven is now its own first step, and the steps say when the pan goes in and comes out. Garlic, rosemary and Parmesan moved from before roasting to after the flip. Pan is oiled before smashing; potatoes are no longer tossed by hand.
  - *Why:* The oven timing was easy to miss, and garlic and cheese could burn over nearly 30 minutes at 425°F. Tossing by hand broke the smashed potatoes.
